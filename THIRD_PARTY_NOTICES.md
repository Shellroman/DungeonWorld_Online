## 5. Legacy source-history note

The Korean public edition identifies the older Dungeon World web text at `book.dwgazetteer.com` as a source. That legacy license page contained an Open Game License history and a specific upstream notice for **Rot Grub** from *Tome of Horrors*, credited to **Necromancer Games, Inc.; Scott Greene and Clark Peterson**, based on original material by **Gary Gygax**.

Dungeon World Online uses the current official Dungeon World CC-licensed source and the Korean public edition as its primary Dungeon World references. This legacy note is retained only to make the older source chain visible where corresponding monster material may appear.

Legacy reference: https://book.dwgazetteer.com/license.html
